# In-game verification plan

Each test settles one or more entries in `conflicts_and_gaps.md`. They are ordered by how much they change the model per minute of effort. All are observation-only: no special tooling, just screenshots or notes while playing. If you send me the screenshots, I do the counting.

| # | Test (setup, what to record) | Settles | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Resource density.** Start 10+ games on a normal Lakes map (any tribes). On turn 1, screenshot the capital. Count tiles in the 8-tile inner ring that carry fruit / crop / animal / metal / fish. | C23, C10, C11 | ~10 min |
| T2 | **Turn-0 snapshot per tribe.** For each tribe you can play: screenshot the tech list, starting unit, stars, capital level, and the score screen on turn 0. | C1-C5, C17, C21 | ~1 min per tribe |
| T3 | **First fog map.** Turn-0 screenshot with the whole revealed area visible, once as a central capital and once as a capital within 2 tiles of a corner. | C21, G9 | 2 min |
| T4 | **Village count.** On several finished or late-game normal-size Lakes maps, count the villages plus cities. | C9 | 5 min per map |
| T5 | **Retaliation.** Warrior (2/2, 10 HP) attacks a fortified Warrior inside an own walled city, then an identical one on open ground. Record HP lost by each side. | C6 | 5 min, needs custom/sandbox game |
| T6 | **Stale fog.** Move a scout next to an enemy unit, step away so the tile leaves vision, and note whether the enemy unit still shows. | G3 | 2 min |
| T7 | **Ocean gate.** With only Fishing (no Sailing), try to move a Raft onto a deep-ocean tile. Check whether starfish are visible with Fishing only. | C8 | 3 min |
| T8 | **Ruins log.** Record the reward every time you open a ruin. | C14, G4 | ongoing |
| T9 | **Bot behaviour.** Save replays of Hard/Crazy games; later extract bot expansion timing and aggression. | G5 | ongoing |
